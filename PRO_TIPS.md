# Pro tips (Minecraft Bedrock, survival)

## Fast start
- First 5 minutes: punch 5 to 8 logs, craft a table and a wooden pickaxe, get 3 cobblestone straight away and make
  stone tools (pickaxe, axe, sword). Never waste time on a wooden sword.
- Sprint-jump to travel faster. Eat before you're below 6 drumsticks so you can keep sprinting.
- Night 1: a bed skips it (3 wool from one colour of sheep + 3 planks). No bed? Dig 2 blocks into a hillside and
  seal the door, mine stone and smelt until morning.
- Food: cooked beef/porkchop are best early. A sheep gives wool AND mutton. Chickens give feathers for arrows.
- Crops: a water block hydrates farmland 4 blocks around it; bone meal grows things instantly.

## Mining
- Diamonds: best at Y -59 (Bedrock). Strip mine with 2-block-tall tunnels and 2 blocks of wall between them.
  Caves and ravines expose ore fast: carry torches and a water bucket.
- Iron: at Y 16 and in mountains; big iron veins between Y -8 and 50. Smelt with coal; a lava bucket smelts 100 items.
- Never dig straight down or straight up. Mine in stairs. Gravel and sand fall: break them from the side.
- Lava: place a block or pour water on it (water + still lava = obsidian, water + flowing lava = cobblestone).

## Gear
- Iron armour set: 24 iron. Shield: 6 planks + 1 iron (blocks arrows and creeper blasts).
- Enchanting: 15 bookshelves around a table (1 block gap) for level 30 enchants. Lapis from Y 0 down.
- Best early enchants: Protection, Sharpness, Efficiency, Unbreaking, Feather Falling, Infinity or Power for bows.
- Water bucket: breaks falls (place it just before landing), puts out fire, stops mobs.

## The Nether
- Portal: 10 obsidian (4 by 5 frame, corners optional), light it with flint and steel. Or build a "bucket portal":
  water bucket + lava bucket, pour lava into a mould and cool it with water, block by block.
- Wear at least one gold armour piece or piglins attack. Barter gold ingots with piglins for ender pearls,
  fire resistance potions, obsidian, string.
- Fortress (dark nether brick): blazes drop blaze rods (you need about 6 to 7). Shields block fireballs; blazes
  are weak to snowballs. Bring food and blocks to bridge.
- Bastions have lots of gold and loot, but brutes hit very hard. Don't break gold blocks next to piglins.
- 1 block in the Nether = 8 in the Overworld: great for fast travel.

## The stronghold and the End
- Eye of ender = blaze powder + ender pearl. Throw one: it flies toward the stronghold. Throw again from further
  on; where they go straight down, dig down in stairs. Eyes sometimes break, so carry 12 or more.
- The end portal needs all 12 frames filled with eyes. Then jump in.
- In the End: build a small pillar right away if you land in the void-side. Don't look endermen in the eyes;
  wearing a carved pumpkin on your head stops them getting angry (it limits your view though).
- Destroy the end crystals on top of the obsidian pillars first (arrows, or pillar up and hit them; caged ones need
  climbing). Each crystal heals the dragon.
- When the dragon perches on the fountain in the middle, hit it hard. Its breath (purple) hurts: step out of it.
- Bedrock trick: beds EXPLODE in the End. When the dragon perches, place a bed next to its head, stand behind a block
  and tap the bed: huge damage. Be careful, it hurts you too. Speedrunners finish the dragon in 2 or 3 bed cycles.
- Bring: bow + lots of arrows, 10+ blocks, a water bucket, food, slow-falling potions if you have them, beds.

## Speedrun route (simple version)
1. Wood, stone tools, food from the first animals.
2. Iron: a bucket, a shield, a flint and steel (iron + flint from gravel).
3. Find lava (surface lava pools or a ravine) and build a bucket portal.
4. Nether: bastion for pearls (barter gold), fortress for blaze rods.
5. Back home, craft eyes of ender, follow them to the stronghold, fill the portal.
6. The End: beds or bow and sword. GG!

## Fun tricks kids love
- Name tags (from fishing, trading, chests) keep pets and mobs forever.
- Tame wolves with bones, cats with raw fish, horses by riding them until hearts appear.
- A sneak-click puts a block where you look without opening chests. Shift on edges to never fall off.
- Bells, note blocks and redstone lamps make cool bases. A daylight sensor turns lamps on at night.
